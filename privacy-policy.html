Privacy Policy for SkyDash

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SkyDash handles information when you use the mobile game. SkyDash is published by NexOra Games.

Information We Collect

SkyDash itself does not require you to directly provide personal information such as your name, home address, or phone number to play the game.

However, SkyDash uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information, including device information, advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, and information related to advertisements.

Advertising

SkyDash uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements. Google and its partners may collect and process information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

You can learn more about how Google handles information at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Services

SkyDash may use Google Play services for features such as purchases, game services, leaderboards, achievements, account-related functionality, and other Google Play features. Google may process information according to its own privacy policies.

In-App Purchases

SkyDash offers optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed through Google Play. SkyDash does not directly receive or store your payment card or banking information.

Game Data

SkyDash may store game-related information such as progress, virtual currency, unlocked content, settings, rewards, and other gameplay information on your device or through supported platform services.

Children's Privacy

SkyDash does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party services used by the game may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with SkyDash. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

SkyDash may use third-party services including Google Play and Google AdMob. These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised when appropriate.
